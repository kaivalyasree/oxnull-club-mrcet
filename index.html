CYBERSECURITY CLUB

OXNULL is a cybersecurity club dedicated to exploring security, building practical skills, and understanding the technologies that protect the digital world.

We encourage curiosity, ethical experimentation, collaborative learning, and hands-on technical growth.

From security testing and digital forensics to cryptography, cloud security, and security architecture, members can explore multiple areas of cybersecurity.

Learn together, develop practical expertise, and turn your interest in cybersecurity into meaningful technical experience.

Specialization Foundations

Seven Cybersecurity Domains

01
Offence & Defence

Red & Blue Team

Two perspectives, one discipline — attack to understand, defend to protect. Every exercise is conducted in controlled labs with documented ethical boundaries.

Explore Domain
02
Investigation & Artefacts

Digital Forensics

Reconstructing events from artefacts — disk, memory and network traces. The discipline of answering "what happened" with evidence, not assumption.

Explore Domain
03
Mathematical Trust

Cryptography

The mathematics that allows two parties to establish trust across an untrusted wire — turning plaintext into ciphertext impenetrable to adversaries.

Explore Domain
04
Infrastructure & Posture

Cloud Security

Most modern systems live in cloud infrastructure. Members learn the shared responsibility model, identity perimeters, and common misconfigurations.

Explore Domain
05
Structural Defense

Security Architecture

Designing systems where security is structural, not bolted-on — threat-modelled, layered, and resilient against evolving threats.

Explore Domain
06
Human & Threat Landscape

Awareness

Reading the threat landscape as it actually moves — beyond sensational headlines, into the mechanics of phishing, social engineering, malware, and emerging attack vectors.

Explore Domain
07
Discovery & Leadership

Explore

A platform where students try different non-technical domains, discover their interests and potential, and choose skills they want to develop further.
